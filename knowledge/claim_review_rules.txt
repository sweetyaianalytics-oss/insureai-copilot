InsureAI Copilot - Synthetic Claim Audit Rules
Educational Prototype Only

These rules are created only for this student capstone. They are NOT the rules of any real insurance company.

Rule 1 - Eligibility
Flag a claim if the service date is before the policy effective date or after the policy expiration date.

Rule 2 - Missing Information
If required information or a required document is missing, send the claim for human review.

Rule 3 - Code Review
If diagnosis and procedure information appears inconsistent based on the prototype test rules, send the claim for human review.

Rule 4 - Duplicate / Anomaly Review
If a duplicate or unusual claim pattern is detected, flag the claim for human audit.

Rule 5 - Human-in-the-Loop
The AI assistant does not make the final payment or denial decision. A human reviewer makes the final review decision.

Rule 6 - Explainability
Every flagged claim should show the reason for the flag and the source/rule used.
